About AD Foundation

Dedicated to bringing free education & technical skills to underprivileged youth.

Our Mission

At AD Foundation, we believe that education is a fundamental right, not a privilege. Our goal is to empower young minds with knowledge, practical skills, and confidence to succeed in today’s modern world.

We offer completely Free Classes ranging from core academic subjects like English, Mathematics, and Science to modern high-tech fields like Artificial Intelligence (AI), Graphic Design, and Computer Basics.

Our Leadership & Founders

Visionaries Behind AD Foundation
